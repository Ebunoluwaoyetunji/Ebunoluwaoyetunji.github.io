KYC & Account Upgrade

Each NexaPay account tier comes with different limits and benefits. Users who needed higher limits or additional benefits had to upgrade their account by providing the required information and documents.

The challenge was handling the number of requirements without overwhelming users.

03

Research

I conducted secondary research to understand how other fintech products approach account upgrades and KYC requirements. I reviewed Raven and OPay to identify common patterns in how account tiers, requirements and verification steps are presented.

While the individual experiences differed, I found that the overall upgrade patterns were fairly consistent across fintech products. I used these patterns as a reference and adapted them to fit NexaPay rather than directly replicating another product.

Tier progression

Users can move to higher account tiers when they need greater limits or additional benefits.

Progressive verification

Requirements can be completed as part of a structured upgrade journey rather than being presented all at once.

Clear requirements

Users need to understand what information or documents are required before continuing.

04

Flow

The account upgrade process required users to provide several details and documents. I mapped the requirements into clear, progressive steps so users could complete the process without feeling overwhelmed.

The flow also helped me define the information needed at each stage before moving into the interface design.

Research→ Flow→ Screens

05

Designing the Experience

Once the flow was defined, I translated each step into the interface using NexaPay's existing design system. I focused on making each stage clear and manageable while maintaining consistency with the rest of the product.

Progressive completion. Rather than presenting every requirement at once, I structured the experience into smaller stages so users could focus on what was needed at each point.

Clear information. I organized the content within each step to help users understand what was required before continuing.

Consistent experience. I worked within the existing NexaPay design system to ensure the new experience felt connected to the rest of the product.
